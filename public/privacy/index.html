Chambeando Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DELIVERYLINK LLC ("DELIVERYLINK," "we," "us") handles information in connection with Chambeando, a peer-to-peer (P2P) crypto/fiat marketplace that you interact with through a WhatsApp Business chatbot. This policy covers Chambeando specifically — it does not describe DELIVERYLINK's other products or websites.

Chambeando is invite-only: you can only use it if someone who is already a member sends you an invitation code.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Through WhatsApp

When you message Chambeando's WhatsApp number, we receive and process, through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform:

  • Your WhatsApp identifier (the wa_id Meta assigns to your chat, which functions as your phone number in international format). We store this to recognize your conversation across messages and to link it to a Chambeando account once you connect a wallet.
  • Conversation state — which step of the bot menu you're on, and small, non-sensitive session context needed to make the menu work (for example, which numbered option you're shown maps to which trade offer). This never includes your message text, settlement details, or wallet private keys.
  • A message identifier for each message Meta delivers to us, kept only to avoid processing the exact same incoming message twice.

We do not log or store the text/content of your WhatsApp messages. Our system is built to record only the message identifier above, never the body of what you typed, in our application logs or database.

1.2 When you connect a wallet and use the marketplace

  • Your public blockchain wallet address, used as your account identity. You prove you control it by signing a one-time login challenge with your wallet — we never see or store your private key or seed phrase.
  • An optional display alias you can choose, shown instead of your full wallet address in parts of the marketplace.
  • Trade/order information tied to your wallet address: crypto amount, declared fiat amount and currency, a generic payment-method label (e.g. "CUP transfer"), and status. Trade escrow itself happens on the TRON blockchain (see section 2) — order metadata you or your counterparty attach is stored on our side.

1.3 Fiat settlement instructions (if you submit them)

If you add payment instructions for receiving fiat currency (for example, account details for a bank/CUP transfer), that content is encrypted before it is stored. It is only ever decrypted for: the specific counterparty of a specific trade you're settling with, or an arbitrator, and only while that trade is under an active dispute. We do not embed these details inside the trade record itself — only a generic payment-method label is stored alongside the order.

1.4 Dispute evidence (if you open or respond to a dispute)

If a trade is disputed, we store the evidence you or your counterparty submit (a note and/or a file) so it can be reviewed by the trade's own parties, the assigned arbitrator, and Chambeando staff handling that specific dispute. It is not visible to other users.

1.5 Security and administrative logs

We keep an internal audit log of account-security-relevant actions — for example, a login attempt, an invite being redeemed, or a membership status change. Each entry records who performed the action, what type of action it was, what it affected, and when — never the sensitive value involved (e.g. never a settlement account number or dispute evidence content).

1.6 What we do not collect

Chambeando does not ask for or store your name, email address, or government ID. Our system has no field for any of these.

2. Third parties involved in providing the service

ServiceWhat it's used for
Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Business Platform) Delivers and receives the WhatsApp messages between you and Chambeando. Meta processes this traffic as the messaging infrastructure provider under its own privacy policy.
TRON blockchain network Chambeando's escrow smart contract runs on TRON. Trade wallet addresses and escrow transactions are recorded on this public, third-party blockchain and are outside our control once submitted — anyone can read on-chain data.

We do not use advertising, analytics/tracking, or data-broker services inside Chambeando, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How long we keep information

We keep the information described above for as long as your Chambeando account or an active conversation/trade needs it to provide the service. We have not yet defined fixed retention periods for each data type; if you'd like something deleted, contact us using the details in section 6 and we will review the request.

4. Security

Fiat settlement instructions you submit (section 1.3) are encrypted at rest. Access to trade, dispute, and settlement data is restricted by role — for example, only a trade's own parties, or an arbitrator during an active dispute, can see decrypted settlement instructions or dispute evidence for that trade.

5. Children

Chambeando is an invite-only financial marketplace intended for adults. It is not directed at children.
