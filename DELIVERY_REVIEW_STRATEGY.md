# Delivery Review Strategy

**Status:** Planning only. No reviews have been requested, posted, or fabricated. This is a process document for the Delivery & Logistics division specifically.

---

## Principle

Every review must come from a real person who actually received a real delivery from DELIVERYLINK LLC. No exceptions, regardless of stage (first review or hundredth).

**Never:**
- Fake reviews
- Purchased reviews
- Review exchanges (you review mine, I review yours)
- Incentivized reviews of any kind Google's policies prohibit (discounts, gifts, or payment in exchange for a review)
- Friends or family posing as customers who never actually used the service

---

## What counts as a real, review-worthy experience

Start logging these *now*, before actively asking for reviews — they double as material for honest case-study content later, separate from the GovCon "Technical Portfolio vs. Past Performance" distinction, which only applies to formal capability statements, not ordinary customer reviews:

- Date of the job
- What was delivered (document, package, supplies — kept general enough not to expose a client's confidential business, unless they're fine with specifics)
- Pickup and drop-off type (business-to-business, government office, etc.)
- Any notable turnaround (only log what actually happened — don't record a "guaranteed" time that wasn't promised)

---

## Milestones

### First 5 reviews
- Ask **immediately** after completing any real, paid delivery — even a single small job for a local business contact counts.
- Ask in person or via a same-day text/email with the direct GBP review link (see `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_PLAN.md` for generating that link).
- Don't batch requests or wait weeks after the job — the closer to the actual delivery, the higher the response rate and the more accurate the review.

### Next 5 (to 10)
- Once there's a small pattern of repeat business-to-business clients, ask each one once, after a completed job.
- Never ask the same client more than once per engagement — repeated asks read as pressure, not a request.

### To 25
- This takes real transaction volume — there is no shortcut. A slow, steady climb to 25 genuine reviews is worth more than any faster path, and Google actively detects and can remove review patterns that look incentivized, exchanged, or family/friend-sourced — which would set the profile back further than starting from zero.

---

## Simple process to follow after every real delivery

1. Complete the delivery.
2. Same day (or next business day at the latest): send a short, personal thank-you message — text or email, not a form letter — with the GBP review link.
3. Do not follow up more than once for the same job.
4. Log the job (date, type, general description) per "What counts" above, whether or not a review results.
5. If a review comes in, thank the reviewer — don't argue with or pressure anyone over a critical review; respond honestly and professionally if a response is warranted.
